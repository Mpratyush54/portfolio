03 / From brief to handover

How an engagement works.

  1. 01

    Send your requirements

    Share the pages, workflows, integrations and timeline you need. A project enquiry is free.

  2. 02

    Review your quotation

    Receive a private, client-specific proposal with exact deliverables, SRS attachments where applicable, exclusions, taxes, total price and the full agreement.

  3. 03

    Approve and pay by milestone

    Accept the agreement explicitly, then pay 25% before onboarding, 25% after MVP approval, 40% after completion approval and 10% after deployment approval.

  4. 04

    Deployment and handover

    Receive the agreed handover after full payment, plus 30 calendar days of agreed-scope defect support from recorded production deployment.
